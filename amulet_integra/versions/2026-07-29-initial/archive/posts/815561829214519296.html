⚙️ Surprising Familiarity with Pop Culture

she fascinates me a little

what kind of person decides “instead of scrolling tiktok, i shall bind myself noumenally to the evershifting zeitgeist”

did she choose thus before christ? in a time and nation where ‘popular culture’ was something completely different? and maintained that connection because she loves it, as much as it’s changed? was it only an errant morning in 1502?

or is it perhaps not a choice at all, but intrinsic property of the vastness to which 'iolithae’ is only voice?

there’s a desperation to it. she’s up to date. she knows in-jokes, even revolting ones from benthic imageboards. she can’t be left out.

most who seek connection lash themselves to people.

and for most, this would be an absurd sublimation of parasociality. patiently listening for hundreds of years to a choir vast and ordinary: the color of perception, all-suffusive and intangible.

but. she can touch it back. she can caress it with only tongue and lips and air.

it demands faith. Lady Zeitgeist, considered as a finite, once-infant process: it is flawed. i need not judge it, for it constantly contradicts and betrays its dearest ideals. seething tides of ideomotion flood the children of man, made utterly certain in concepts and beliefs that are the next day neglected dross. a mundane peril to all that lives long: yet she is inherently less insular than any human.

a walking boundary, thin and fleeting as gossamer. when you try to date a mermaid, most of what you get is ocean.

…i find it very cute how she has to watch matinees if she doesn’t want to be spoiled. which means despite being naturally one of the most informed people, she also needs to keep tabs on culture manually. and her day job is destroying Creation.

it’s also nice that you could communicate with her exclusively through say, epistolary allegorical MCU fanfiction, if you wanted to do that for some reason. whatever you’re most comfortable with
