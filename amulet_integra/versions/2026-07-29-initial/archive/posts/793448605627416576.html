concubine-hypergreen

you were created by and exist solely within Her Resplendence’s right hand. Her fingers have drawn serpentine grooves into your identity, massaging you into the form most pleasing to Her eye. you bow in worship of Her every palm line.

when golden dawn tastes the mouthful of the world containing you and your eyes shutter open from prayer — your vessel is filled with a lattice of clarity, fostered within it the vinework of love. no word can be said to define you more than service, and this is an elation beyond any other.

you are no meager plaything for Her to teach only suffering and disposal. She has seen a glimmer of competence in you and set you as keystone over a delegation of other dolls.

a fantasy i have about you

■

newfound in its service, this one accidentally destroys an object of sentimental value to you. careful as you are, it had to err in multiple ways for this to occur. you negotiate the claw of fury in your heart down to frigid, professional disdain. it is only months old. you instruct it to wait in its room while you clean up the mess.

it repents prolifically when you go to retrieve it. you stare into the dull white of its hair. it stares into the lustrous black of your boots. eventually you leave, without punishment or rebuke. perhaps you simply had no words for it, or your thoughts were clouded with other matters — yet it receives the silence you share with it as an unthinkable beneficence. it spends the night with the impression of a creature of oceanic dignity and mercy.

it spends the next few days too frightened to touch anything for fear of destroying more — save only stone and water. thus it does not do its chores or wear its uniform. others assume it has run away in shame.

instead it watches you work, scarce and nude from darkened closets and balconies above. you catch flashes of it but say nothing — new servants often learn by watching, and you are one of the better role models it might emulate. its eyes are glassy dark and seem to comprehend nothing, like a pigeon watching a train.

it ceases to hide from you by gradients, at first slipping away at the sound of any other person — then finally it follows openly at a duckling’s pace, once again uniformed and demure.

seeming guilty of its idle hands, it scurries to hold doors for you. it offers its arms to carry your things. it begins bringing items for your chores and routines beforehand: a cup of water, a cup of window cleaning solution, a screwdriver. when a passing guest drapes a big coat over its head, it grips your skirts lightly so it can continue to follow.

another doll has already taken up its previous bed and chores. it seems to want nothing but to continue running your errands. it transcribes your letters, delivers messages, fetches and carries. it seems to delight in having bags looped over it and boxes and books stacked in its arms. loyalty has bloomed inside it, threaded with reverence - but shadowed with cold terror of being reassigned with a bemused smile.

you become accustomed to the presence two short steps back and to the right. any item held behind your back vanishes, swiftly returning when reached for once again. it becomes habituated to couching the sound of its shoes within yours, and startles at the hollowness when it has to walk quietly on its own. it seems less alien now, a pigeon perched on the window of a train car.

sometimes it plays games with you. clinging tightly enough to your waist to restrict your movement. bringing you increasingly wrong tools to complete a task, five times in a row. you are patient. it corrects itself soon after, and its devotion seems renewed.

during formal events and private affairs, it waits in your room curled up in a laundry basket, clinging to your skirts even still. as though out of guilt, it always tries to offer some form of entertainment whenever you return — a toy or bauble it has brought, a half minute of dance, a sketch of something it’s learned you like.

it could hardly be more willing to let you tinker with it, in body and mind. you build on what you find appealing in it and smooth away what you do not. you unwind its strings to lay them personally, tuning each as you see fit. you match its internal clock and technical standardizations to your own. when part of it wears down or snaps, it is always you who it kneels to in supplication for repair, not your mutual lady.

it behaves exactly as if your property, replacing the thing it once broke.

it stitches your name on the inside of its uniform’s left sleeve (which you soon notice) and the underside of its righthand thigh panel (which stays secret a little longer).

it blends droplets of dye in different concentrations until it recreates the color of your hair exactly; it dyes its own. it paints its lips and face to mirror yours. when your hairstyle changes, so shifts its own. when it fails, you stand behind it and wordlessly redo its mistakes.

its internals require comprehensive changes to permit it speech. it seems to consider your voice as something distinct from what others use, a separate category: graceful and clear rather than abrasive and meaningless. it emulates your voice as closely as it can.

the first few times you display an idiosyncracy, it signs a request for you to hold or loop it. the exchange pauses a half minute to allow this. it is here its eyes are most fierce and intent, while coiling a strand of hair about its finger in the exact same langorous, musing curve.

on lesser tasks that would call for your presence — it has made itself a useful delegate, piloting itself to avoid any visible discrepancy. even Her eyes in all their subtlety detect no flaw in its poise. you are a role that it subsumes itself in, and does so fully.

it is permitted the height of honor when entrusted with the responsibility of minding your uniform, the manifestation of your beauty and dignity itself. it starts to use ‘she’ sometimes, after that.

it dresses you. cold hands cloak and adorn a cold body in the softness of cloth. it sheathes your hands within their gloves, fingertips smoothing away every tiny crease.

it washes your clothes beside its own at night, in dark waters reflecting the glassy moon. it mends them. when gloves stretch or shoes widen, it is there. its world seems to exist as a diagram of you and all things directly related to you.

on each night after it has erred, it lays before you and exposes sensitive joints you might snap, twist, or crush. its neck, its jaw, its fingers, its ankles, its wrists.

no longer is its loyalty improved by kindness. it has already decided that you are the source of all light, warmth, and beauty in the world. whatever you do to it, it knows, is correct.
