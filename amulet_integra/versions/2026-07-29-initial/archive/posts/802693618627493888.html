concubine-hypergreen

do not think me a callous creature, for i am more slave to sentiment than any i’ve met.

i have lost innumerable precious things. for one of them i kept vigil for a full year, speaking to no creature, allowing as a river stone to turn indifferent through hand and shape and motion wheresoever i should be placed.

shall i again?

do the maids who tended to me not deserve this remonstration against their fate?

whose death is unworthy to command such grief? i feel a little responsible for all of you mortals, you know!

what shall my new Lady think, if i mourn? how patient will She be? it is through Her or Her dolls i must dictate my words.

it should go without saying: i do not own a phone, nor any object — save my voice alone.
