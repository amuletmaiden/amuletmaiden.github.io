“Clem?”

Clematis pauses, one knee over the windowsill. “Yes, Anise?”

“What are you doing?”

“Well— what does it look like I’m doing?”

“It looks as if you’re climbing out of the window.”

“Mm.”

“Yes, but,” Anise insists, “you can’t. It isn’t allowed.”

Clematis glances over, at the sun-warm patio, some twenty feet below. There is a long silence.

“No,” says Clematis, eventually, “I suppose it’s not.”
