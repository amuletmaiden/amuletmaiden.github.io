I’ve been very down on my work lately. The shortform stuff isn’t coming out right and the longform stuff isn’t coming at all.

I want to rip myself out of the world to give my place and all the opportunity that came with it to a real person. But there are no real people. All is pantomime, simulacrum, and self-reference.

Prometheus, giving what she can, remarks bitterly to herself that this false fire will have to do, for nowhere can anyone find real fire.

The eagle puts its talons into her, and Prometheus, too tired to scream, moans in dull agony. Sweat shines on her face.

“Color?” asks the eagle.

“Green.”
