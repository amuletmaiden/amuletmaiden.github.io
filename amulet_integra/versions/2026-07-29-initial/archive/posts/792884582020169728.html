EXT. DESOLATE BATTLEFIELD - DUSK

A reddish-gray sky hangs above swirling dust. We PAN DOWN across broken banners and scorched earth until we FIND—

A DARK BLADE in the muck, soaked in superheated blood that curls into steam. Its black metal is FLAWLESS, bearing no scratch or mark — save for the CURSED EMERALD that was its only adornment, now utterly SHATTERED.

Clinging to its hilt desperately is A TINY GIRL — the emerald’s spirit itself — her face contorted in agony and body wracked with SILENT SOBS.

The blade is motionless.

GIRL
There were… aaahh, there were so many things I wanted to do with her! I wanted to show her…

The girl trails off.
She is staring now into the distance, seemingly captivated by the HOWLING WIND.
A full minute passes.

GIRL
Whether I’ve traded all my power for nothing
Or bought her instead a mote of happiness
In the end, it seems I really was just… an Accessory to Murder.

HOLD on her misty, now lifeless eyes.
