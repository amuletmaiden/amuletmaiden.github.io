i had Miss Cupbearer stop on a walk today because she lost her glasses and then i saw a plastic comb. i’m pretty sure it’s magic.

i expect it is specifically an instrument of doll edification which can invert despair.

my dark lady Amaryllis says i’m really observant and smart. i told her she can’t sell it.

she says it needs to be adorned with a hundred perfect beads of crystallized blood, each harvested from a different enemy, to unlock its latent power

that’s scary. i don’t have that many enemies

maybe i can find enemies of something no one likes and set up a blood drive.

but wouldn’t it be sad if there was a thing no one liked? i feel compassion for that thing!

i don’t know what to do… but i won’t give up on dreams!
