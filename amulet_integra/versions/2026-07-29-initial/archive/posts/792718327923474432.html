concubine-hypergreen

about

the emerald lies upon the windowsill, drinking the shimmer of midday’s last light as an ocean sips of river. the sunbeam sifts by refraction into its gloaming wells, thinning and winnowing as it travels a distance that only light can.

cold and pale it falls over the face of the girl at its heart, suspended within the verdant bloom of a million rays mingled.

to the stranger’s eye, she is pinioned eternal by the facets of the emerald world. all range of motion is demarcated by still, artificial lines. her wings broaden only two-thirds of their length before pressing full against cold, angular contour.

but to her, there is only slenderest distinction between the stone part of the world and the shadow that tends to it.

■

she studies the banners of light that stream through her prism form or fall nearby. she studies dust in descent. she is placed in boxes where she reflects on darkness, and dropped in pouches where she reflects on motion.

each time she catches glimpse of another gem, she closes her eyes and tries to engrave the image on the back of her eyelids. she wonders if they make an effort to remember her too.

for abrupt, fleeting slivers of time, she is lifted. encompassed within majestic hands that touch and turn her. these too she forcibly sears into her memory, as stimulation to reflect upon over and over until drained to blur.

she learns to differentiate their fingerprints by tactility. at times, they are wreathed in cloth as she is cradled in stone. this makes it more difficult to interpret what is happening to her outside face, but does not at least blemish with trace oils.

for longer, langourous moments — luxuriate, ephemeral, as though blinking amidst a dream — she is worn. shining at the crest of a golden crown. bright upon a warm delicate neckline.

this is something she prizes. brighter than the reflections she sees in other gems, more of a freefall than falling itself. to be adored. glorified.

■

in the clearing of her heart sits the understanding that she is a maiden — one who exists to maintain the world. to care for and to edify.

yet she cannot herself wash away the dust that at times sprinkles the gem’s surface, nor polish its lustre, nor seal it in everlasting sanctuary. just as soon could the universe turn itself inside out as she could trace her lips over the lines of her glimmering outside face.

the tools that avail themselves to hand: her minute form, her encapsulated voice, and the light in which she is cast.

she turns inward. she defines symbols with which to interpret the world (stone, shadow, adoration) and ways to express those symbols. when those outside speak, it reaches her only as a dull warm murmur - she emulates the sound.

with this, she turns herself wholly upon the task of glorification. serving the gem with her love. adoring it as she was adored. she fills her mind with poems that flourish into hymns that flower into soliloquies. she fills the chamber of the emerald world with her voice.

she impresses her worship into the interior facets that cradle her with delicate, reverent kisses. she sings gently to the outside facets, adamant against dust and smears and all else that defiles. she sings of the loveliness of the shadow, ever-resolute to exalt and reify.

■

she finds herself flecked with blood again. this happens every other often: she is violently stolen away, only to be fiercely guarded as before, but now in new hands.

she reflects upon herself as an object of desire. she is the most valuable thing that she has ever known and ever will. a thing that commands need, that defines and forms the cusp of ambition itself. that others find their very lives to be dross before the face of. a symbol of power — which as symbols do, gives the world meaning.

this, she thinks, is wonderful. when her possessors gaze into her gleaming depths, she whispers sweet allurance. faint, as though an echo passed through long caverns. only in total stillness might it be heard, softer than a single trembling leaf.

“this is your reward for owning me,” she sings. “look upon the fullness of my beauty,” she sings, “and see the breadth of my adoration for you. all that i am exists as a testament to your glory. own me forever!”

she turns her love honed over ages upon her owners. they bend like flowers underfoot. every word of it is sincere, for each time she has true faith in they who vanquished the last.

individual hands blur together, and she speaks to them as manifestations of their role itself, with whom she shares a long history. the Other. the Conqueror. the Counterpart.

her words are heeded. yet even so they seem fated to fumble and die, much to her chagrin. once she is even, astoundingly, misplaced.

until finally, she finds herself in the hands of a grand and magnificent witch. Her stately hands run cold with sweat. they tremble like a schoolgirl’s. She is pierced by the jewel’s beauty in a way that no blade has ever known.

“I’ll take you away,” She whispers. “into the formless dark. where you won’t have the slenderest hope of escape, nor I. not by loss. not by decay. you will know no hand but Mine!”

“then i will love You there,” says the jewel. “until it is formless light.”
