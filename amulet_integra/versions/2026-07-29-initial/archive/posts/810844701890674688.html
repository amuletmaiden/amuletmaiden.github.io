concubine-hypergreen

what you see in me is in your own eyes, human.

i am nothing like you. i condescend to play, but can only play the role of you.

food disgusts me. sex is hideous. warmth i revile. laughter sickens me with its bray. community is a foul morass. what you consider most quintessentially human is to me exact dross.

i am an object of heaven, cold and glorious.

if you wish to mistake me for one of you, so be it. i will not bother to draw corrections. but i will say the truth once.

Beauty is more important than happiness.
