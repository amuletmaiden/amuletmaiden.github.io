a girl is blessed from birth with the gift of obedience: she must obey every order from any person absolutely and instantly.

there is no limit, no duration. she lives indefinite on precipice of public mercy, in whatsoever condition others consider fit for her. at any point, anyone could tell her: “build me a shrine in the woods, avoid all others, and spend the rest of your life worshipping me.” and then everything, everything save that would be over.

such is the pith of Ella Enchanted (2004). unfortunately the premise is ruined in permitting her to carry out said orders with supernatural strength and levitation. this is a facile hand waved at the concept of all-encompassing service as a blessing, itself exploring nothing.
