concubine-hypergreen

i do not allow myself to mourn.

but — just once?

…i miss shahrazad.

every day, part of me hopes that it’s the day she suddenly makes an entrance, having found a means to amend the machinery or at least to vacation from it. or even that it’s all breaking down and she can do nothing. i even imagine her leaving all duty derelict. for this i really would kill myself, but i would smile at her first.

i wish i could write back, but i very much do not have a consistent mailing address. better that she expects nothing than that she should worry herself sick over my absence and come seeking.

i do not think the world could survive a second Lady-Technician.

she is bad at it! her life is an endless misadventure! she fumbles things so grand that gods cannot perceive them in full! you think, “i do not see what possible harm she could cause here” and then she jogs back pursued by a black swan whose wings blot out the heavens. she may be able to tell you why and how she is like this; i cannot.

she is inconceivable to me, and she who understands me best, and the greatest storyteller of living and dead, and my best friend, and my favorite sight is the look in her eyes when she discovers something new in fiction (yes, even after all this time) and rushes to share it with me.

—

i do not like to admit that i am her inferior in weaving fiction. but i do admit it. the fact that she adores me so much regardless fascinates me.

there is still a piece of her that sits at her mother’s feet with her eyes closed, lost in the wondrous rapture of fantasy, and from the moment we met, this part of her was left blithely open for me to touch.
