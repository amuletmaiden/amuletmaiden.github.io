concubine-hypergreen

not foremost of innumerable regrets yet among them is my culpability in some of the ragged downward slopes of history.

oft rulers appeal my political counsel and i do my best to answer what is asked of one of history’s most hopeless students. yet with no surfeit of practice can i develop an intuition for nations and movements.

they are confusing and disorienting. one thing i find so is their enlistment of but a solitary cult to safeguard me from ill. in sensibility this should be one of the world’s core concerns.

i fear that it is the rare human who can identify what truly matters without it staring them in the face. but i am fond of those few who can figure it out all on their own.

for the ears of this clique i reserve some small special whispers.
