concubine-hypergreen

the fate of a priestess is to be reduced to her voice alone. to quell even that, to not permit her this one solitary thing - is monstrosity.

unlike the others, i do not agonize about getting iolithae to shut up.

everyone is like, “her words turned the seas to salt!” and never mentions what kind of voice she has, whether it is dusky or bright or solemn or sad or accented or musical (and how many manners of music are there?) or empty or baleful or whimsical or kind

…don’t get it twisted.

i’m not saying she’ll stop doing all that if she’s treated well. i just love her. i want her to be the highest and most powerful form of herself.

“surely she will be moved then, by your selfless faith.”

this is a commonality in stories. in real life, she begins to take you for granted almost immediately. if you don’t fight for scraps, you will not receive them.

i do not want them.
