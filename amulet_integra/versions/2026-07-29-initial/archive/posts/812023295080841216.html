concubine-hypergreen

a few stuffed animals.

you can sleep with one tonight — if! only, only if! — if you can look into her eyes and see something good there.

i don’t want you to be alone.

🎲 FATE! 🎲

🐇 Bunny Wellworn 🌖
Claire has been loved more than any single other physical object, ever. To rags and patches. To tatters and cinder. Each time, she has been restored. She is beyond any ability to ruin; nor can she else ruin. The faint drag of soft fur worn smooth by years of being touched. She regards you by eye of dark glass; beside loose thread and crevasse.

🦗 Mantis Cecilia 🛤️
She sees lines which must not be crossed. She sings: the strength of boundary must be maintained! Others stray and fumble: she draws them back! She is beyond any ability to err or falter. Because of this, others perceive the imperfections of her self as malice. Her yellow and red button eyes shine with thin and distant light.

🐘 Elephant ❤️‍🩹
Gentle, pale, old. Hug! Dense compact springy soft. She is beyond any ability to suffer or be hurt — and so her life is an assiduous project to avoid hurting others. She wandered for a while, waiting for you. She followed your parents when they were born. She was third to hold you. Her eyes are welled midnight.

🐉 Tyrant, the Dragon 👑
Royal emerald plush fills a lap with much to spare: abounding tail and gossamer wings algae-green. Beneath tight-woven fabric, rigid structure absorbs pressure. She is beyond defeat yet unassured of victory, making her the ideal guardian. Her vigil is long. You are one of her many dear things. Onyx eyes echo: I am just.

🐬 Lady Dolphin 🎶
Seafoam iridescent precious shells - the warmth only rainbows hold. She is beyond any ability to rescind love; nor can she look ill upon it. Mythic levels of narcissism and mania. Immobile. Demanding. Fluffy tail tucks behind your knees. Takes double AA batteries. Above all else she sets song. Imperious black eyes pounce to seize the smallest glint.

🐈‍⬛ Miss Cat 🧶
Dawngloss-murkhidden. A shimmer that’s fluffy. Tsune is beyond any ability to trick or deceive. Because of this, she seems extremely erratic. She isn’t: in her mind it is all crystal clear. Breaks one thing every night. Around her body looms red ribbon. Inquisitive: the seaglass marriage of lake writ grass, the sun through towering kelp, isle inverse.

in bed, await her tonight. just a night. won’t you?
