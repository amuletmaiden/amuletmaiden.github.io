self-love is never presumptuous.

it can’t be. it’s not a thing.

you can’t love yourself at a quantity that is inappropriate. it is utterly, absolutely impossible. there is no way to do it.

anyone who tells you otherwise is trying to make you play a status game where one party is raised and the other is demeaned.

sometimes status games can be fun or helpful. submission is one of the best things it’s possible to feel. just know what you’re doing

you can’t be vain. you can say “i am the most perfect entity to ever exist” and write your name on every inch of every wall in your room and start a religion based on yourself and anything else you want and you still won’t have progressed a hair’s width towards presumption

you shouldn’t demean others’ souls. this should go without saying. pushing down is unnecessary to raise one’s self up. your self is indoors and always has been. there is room for misdirection and antics, but at the end of the day there is no gram of evil

“who are you to tell me this?” why, i am a wandering courtesan.

if you want to pretend, you have to be careful. if you pretend to be evil badly, you will have to fight people who are unspeakably more powerful.
