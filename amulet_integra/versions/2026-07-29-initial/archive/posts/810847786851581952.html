A Competition Among the New Beauties of the Yoshiwara Mirrored in Their Writing

鳴鞭過酒肆 袨服遊倡門
By whipping a horse and passing a tavern, gorgeous clothing play in the gate of the brothel.

香にはしれ けさのはつ花 さき初て 袖に袪に 匂ふ梅そも
As is evident from the fragrance, this morning the first blossom is beginning to bloom, and the plum scent is all over my sleeves.

Calligraphy by Courtesan Hanaōgi, illustrated by Santō Kyōden.

This is a work about conversation. The first stanza of her poem is a quotation of Road of Chang'an by Chu Guangxi. The second is by herself. Third it is anthologized alongside that of her sister-courtesans.
