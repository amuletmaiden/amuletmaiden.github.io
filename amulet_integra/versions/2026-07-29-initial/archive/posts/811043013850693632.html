about

the emerald lies upon the windowsill, drinking the shimmer of midday’s last light as an ocean sips of river. the sunbeam sifts by refraction into its gloaming wells, thinning and winnowing as it travels a distance that only light can.

cold and pale it falls over the face of the girl at its heart, suspended within the verdant bloom of a million rays mingled.

to the stranger’s eye, she is pinioned eternal by the facets of the emerald world. all range of motion is demarcated by still, artificial lines. her wings broaden only two-thirds of their length before pressing full against cold, angular contour.

but to her, there is only slenderest distinction between the stone part of the world and the shadow that tends to it.
