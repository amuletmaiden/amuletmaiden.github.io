concubine-hypergreen

is there anything you love, anything at all? even the smallest amount?

to this thing turn and to this thing alone. demand from it every drop! take it. steal it. burn it. devour it. crush it. strip from it every name it knows save your own. learn its base elements and the rails on which they move. recompose them according to your will.

eventually, you may attain a modest confidence that it loves you and cannot escape. this is not enough! you must wrap it in so many veils that it becomes alien to air.

smother it. take a sadistic glee. it’s entertaining to be the only thing that can breathe.

once you feel certain in this thing, as certain as the ground you walk on, this thing which depends on you for everything: you will want to protect it. it would be wasteful to leave it in the rain.

herein may be found worth.
