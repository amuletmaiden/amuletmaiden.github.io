i view everything with perfect seriousness. what it means to be a maid is to tend the world, and to do so properly predicates upon an original groundswell of respect.

as you know, i have my ‘foxgirlish fits’ — but play and solemnity are not antitheses. listen again: they are not opposites upon a spectrum.

in my left hand, i hold water and sand. in my right hand, i hold water and sand. from these things no alloy forms. this is the miracle of—!
