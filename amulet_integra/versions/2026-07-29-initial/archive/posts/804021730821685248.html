concubine-hypergreen

if i could wear shoes, they would be rainbow light-up sneakers with retractable rollerblades.

they would also have wings that flutter or curl to express their emotions.

and i would force other women to be as reverent about them as the most insane bootblacking doll.

—

no surprise is thus when one considers that in likeness to noble miss Flowers, i too am subject to seven+ evil exes.

apropos: i have never worn shoes before, or a different dress. my feet don’t callus, if you’re interested in that sort of thing.

my sneakers they’d need a whole maid just to take care of them. she’d tie my laces for me while i smile guilelessly at her even though i know how.

“is this what you spend your days thinking about, miss Cintamani?”

imaginary scenarios? i mean… yes? philosophy and mathematics are also imaginary scenarios, and frankly i don’t know what isn't—

—

i can sweat with active effort, though stasis reasserts when i stop paying attention. my wings are functional: no surprise since shadows are weightless.

i can also do heart eyes and sparkle: you should understand that these are subtle actuators of self-expression, not once disproportionate or droll. it is as facet of reality that Lady-Courtesan Cintamani is elegant in all things.

—

“do you mean to suggest that rainbow skates with wings and lights and their own feelings are elegant?”

correct. and were we face-to-face, hypothetical interlocutor, i would discuss this with you until you apologized sincerely to them, complete with a kiss, and learned to be sweet.
