🐇 Bunny Wellworn 🌖

🐘 Elephant ❤️‍🩹
Gentle, pale, old. She is beyond any ability to suffer or be hurt — and so her life is an assiduous project to avoid hurting others. She wandered for a while, waiting for you. She followed your parents when they were born. She was third to hold you. Her eyes are welled midnight.

🐉 Tyrant, the Dragon 👑
Deep full cobalt plush flecked with silver, crowned with dark glass eyes. She fills a lap with much to spare, with a thick tail and gossamer wings. She is beyond defeat yet unassured of victory, making her the ideal guardian. Her vigil is long. You are one of her many dear things.

🐬Lady Dolphin 🌈
Seafoam iridescent precious shells - the warmth only rainbows hold. She is beyond any ability to rescind love; nor can she look ill upon it. Mythic levels of narcissism and mania. Soft. Immobile. Tender to the humble. Fluffy tail tucks behind your knees. Double AA batteries. Above all else she sets song. Her black eyes seize light.

🐈‍⬛ Miss Cat 🧶
She is gorgeous and clever. A shimmer that’s fluffy. Sara is beyond any ability to trick or deceive. Because of this, she seems extremely erratic. She isn’t: in her mind it is all crystal clear. Breaks one thing every night.
