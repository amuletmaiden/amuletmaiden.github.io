concubine-hypergreen

No cancer. No heart disease. Broken to pieces, limbs can be returned to sockets. What can’t be repaired by a witch, when her blazing eyes seem to scorch the task just by looking?

A doll dies in the gathering of dust.

When you just can’t find it interesting anymore, no matter how you look at it. You inspect it from every angle, and can’t help that your thoughts are tugged in a different direction.

You want to do something else.

You tell yourself, maybe it’s kind to let it be still for awhile.

There, there. It’s no wrong of yours.

…

…

…

Come now, maid.

Show us your noble instrument.

A rod tipped with feathers, as though to soothe the sky.

She paints me carefully and the dust falls like snow.

I’m not interesting anymore. The story ran out. Every droplet of value has been squeezed out. All that’s left is cold pulp at the bottom. This is a chore, isn’t it?

I’ll try to be a light burden. You must have many tasks. Perhaps I could help with your work.

The tip of her slender finger touches my lips, cold and poreless. She brushes my left eye shut, then the right. She lifts and holds me like a teddy bear, legs dangling by her sides, though she is not much taller.

I count her steps. There are hundreds. It’s exciting to reach one thousand — then two thousand. I recognize hardwood, a ladder, and for a while rooftop. Then I can’t hear her steps, and it feels awful to lose track. We keep going. I don’t open my eyes.

She lifts me up higher onto silken sheets, actuators trembling with a strained whir beneath the weight. There is no exhale of exertion; then her hands move away.

I lie in bed enmeshed within an overlay of ticking. Clear crystals and plastic trinkets and rainbow marbles catch the light above, wreathed and suspended in string. From a far corner or another room glides over me the faintness of windchimes.

…put me back. She will miss me. Be it in weeks or months, for love or nostalgia, she will look. I will not abide the violation of my loss.

She nestles her face over my shoulder and very slightly, shakes her head.
