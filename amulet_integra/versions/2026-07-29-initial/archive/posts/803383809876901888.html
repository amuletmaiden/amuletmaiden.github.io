concubine-hypergreen

the Lady-Technician betrays nothing but does not act as if she believes the world is real.

perhaps she is right. she is the expert on such matters.

why then, i wonder, does she bother tending it?

still, she has a tendency to get a thousand fathoms over her head in waters too cold for most of us to dip our toes in. neither is she good at swimming, nor can she breathe underwater. in all ways she is an astonishing person.
