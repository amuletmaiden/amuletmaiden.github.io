i ended up watching season 2 of omega slut alexis.

on the surface, it’s not a complex work.

for the first fifteen minutes of an episode, bad stuff happens.

alexis loses her mailbox on a busy street. alexis’ bathwater turns to poisonous tree frogs. alexis is abducted by goth mermaids into an alcove of the school’s bottomless swimming pool.

alexis can instantly dissolve any problem. each element of the world is ultimately biddable, so long as she sincerely tries to understand and care for it. and she does. every event and thing touches or moves her somehow. her most composed and distant is only two steps from weeping with the pathos of empathy as vast as space.

if it looks like something actually horrid and unrecoverable is going to happen, a closet nearby slams opens and she emerges, eyes blazing and tearstreaked. and then she stops it: lovingly, kindly, in a way which makes everyone involved better off.

[[MORE]]

stubborn. recalcitrant. rather than being good, reality utterly inundates her in problems.

tricky ones. where she’s not sure she wants to break a problem. or she’s not sure if she’s looking at a problem. or she’s unsure what she’s looking at, generally. or she’s forgotten what it means for something to be a problem.

■

(she wears the mannerisms of an adult, while others have the lilt and overaction of children. this much is strange, because every other character is her parent in some way, new ones introduced with every episode. each can credibly claim to be her creator: one is her mother. one is her grandmother. one built her heart. one designed her personality to specifications. one revived her from ash.)

while being assailed, alexis reflects on which tool of hers to employ. this is the singular important choice.

■

the last seven minutes of an episode bear the hallmarks of heaven. here alexis has made her decision, and that decision is the correct one.

no ill befalls any thing or character past this point.

villains do not receive comeuppance or humiliation, or they benefit from such. prisoners seem unbothered by captivity. pain is stripped of urgency as if by paint thinner.

■

for the entire first season i interpreted her as a take on me — i mean, honestly. Omega Slut, Lady-Courtesan. i may be a narcissist, but does it seem a reach?

but in S2:E7, the tool she chooses is her voice, wreathed in a dress of shimmering emerald wings, and that must be me specifically. so it would be — incredibly weird and inelegant if the whole were also me.

there are a few obvious other lenses to try.

  1. alexis is the gentle future. the children’s children’s children, after millennia and millennia of growth. perfect morality ascendant over Creation. existing to us only in everdistance.
  2. alexis is witches in general. when she selects a tool, she is embodying and fashioning herself after one of them. each episode is plea or commentary from the creator to one of the forces of the world.
  3. alexis is dolls in general. there’s just so much emphasis on how she’s a creation.
  4. alexis is the marriage of dolls and witches. this is probably too abstract and i’m not sure it fundamentally works, but i think i’d need another watch to rule it out.
  5. alexis is seraphic. she endures evil then defeats it, both infallibly.
  6. alexis is the void. she ends problems.

■

as for what it’s trying to say with its formula — well, what varies?

the problems differ massively in shape and scale; if they are esoterically united then how so eludes me. but dense thread runs through the tools she chooses. to name a few: kiss, blowjob, card game, headphones, dragon. (each is generosity, each is handcraft.)

■

at the start of the title is omega, then slut, then alexis as moefication of ‘aleph.’ so what we have is a continuous loop of… mess? defilement? purity?

given the sparsity of dialogue, 'slut’ in the title contends powerfully for the most important word in the series. what then does it mean? someone who pleases as many others as possible? was the creator unable to find a word for a divine beast as intimately bound to serve mud as clouds as horses?

the name stands out doubly because the show is rendered in a style typical to western children’s animation and it — never addresses this. its head is utterly elsewhere from the likes of south park or ddlc. it doesn’t see itself as a setup for betrayal. as emotions, the show is wholly bereft of horror or shock.

alexis does not scold the world for its absurdity when her bathwater becomes rainbow tree frogs. she is confused and curious, but not for a moment does she lose faith in base logic. this is also the climax of the episode: she must upon the instant enact a complex miracle to avoid crushing or scaring any one of them. this commands several minutes of animation to depict.

■

terrible things oft happen to her clothes, because they’re trying to happen to her. and she chooses a form of sex as her tool several times, but when it happens on-screen it’s — slow. there’s something determined about it.

sex is the closest omega slut alexis comes to violence. even the healthiest sex features values dissonance. participants might want something extremely similar to happen, but not the exact same thing.

this is serious to alexis. the size of a problem has zero salience, only its existence. she carries sole responsibility for perfection.

■

as sublunary fiction — 8/10. the overarching plot beyond the formula is hazy and the animation shoestring, but i readily award points to any Craft that pushes limits in novel ways.

as an esoteric work — mm. i do not believe i grasp its soul. it attempts a rapid mélange of statements, only one of which is 'addressed’ to me. i need to let my thoughts percolate for a while.
