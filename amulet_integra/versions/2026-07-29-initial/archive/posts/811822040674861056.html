welcome to dark academia kindergarten

we wander fathomless stone halls and cradle juicebox.

in sickly radiance and benthic dark traverses a hidden axis. it is pure: you believe this is a lie but you do not know how. you stalk it under slivers of the pale dying moon of 6:30pm. for your love you risk being lost forever.

you have one stuffed animal. it is the whole of solace in the world.

your peers grovel and debase themselves before false spirits Semblance of Orange and Apple Necrose. you know a higher light. it is one that burns you.

you cannot count above ten.
