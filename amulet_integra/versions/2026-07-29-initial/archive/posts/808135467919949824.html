WTF presents a world that supplants classical logic with its own ‘counterculture’ engine. It runs on sophistry enforced by various self-oriented gatekeepers, and if you engage and entertain them for a little time, they will become bemused or enamored with your ideas instead and allow you to do whatever it was you wanted. Especially if you sound poetic while interacting with them.

Further: it only offers the illusion of coherency. What facts are true about the world? Well, the Weaver will decide when you ask them, like laying pieces of rail in front of a train. What facts are true about those facts? Same thing.

Oh, the rules don’t tell you how to do something? Heaven forfend that rules be omitted or poorly specified. It sure is fortunate that the rules contain toys through which you can achieve actions within the rules.

It dances with absurdity’s shawl as a way of veiling its basic allegory, shining from the pages like a flashlight in your face if you know what you’re looking at

…on a personal level the fact that it’s modern Cintamani RPF serves only somewhat to elevate it in my eyes
