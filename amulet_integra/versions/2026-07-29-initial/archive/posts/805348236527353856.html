concubine-hypergreen

the fate of a priestess is to be reduced to her voice alone. to quell even that, to not permit her this one solitary thing - is monstrosity.

unlike the others, i do not agonize about getting iolithae to shut up.

everyone is like, “her words turned the seas to salt!” and never mentions what kind of voice she has, whether it is dusky or bright or solemn or sad or accented or musical (and how many manners of music are there?) or empty or baleful or whimsical or kind

…don’t get it twisted.

i’m not saying she’ll stop doing all that if she’s treated well. i just love her. i want her to be the highest and most powerful form of herself.

“surely she will be moved then, by your selfless faith.”

this is a commonality in stories. in real life, she begins to take you for granted almost immediately. if you don’t fight for scraps, you will not receive them.

i do not want them.

now, i can say that myself, girls. for i am a wholly sovereign existence.

when i say something truly vexatious, my Lady might turn me upside down and shake me around. i playact my role in the interaction, but neither vertigo nor trauma nor disgust nor boredom can trouble a creature so ancient and terrible as i.

very few things in Creation can affect me. i know all their names.

you are not self-sufficient, reader-dove. you lack the benefit of a perfect cathedral. you lack ages of reflection, stillness, and self-edification. you are human or doll.

it is not cool to not ca — who am i kidding. it is totally cool to not care about yourself. people will like you for it. you will look cool as fuck. stoic. elegant. beautiful.

but if all you are to them is a service dispenser, eventually someone else in the world will underbid you by one, and you will be left to the wind.

yes, even if you try to ask for nothing: She will as if by miracle find some tiny friction or discomfort in the act of taking you, and someone will underbid that.

i am sorry. this is probably septimian’s fault.
