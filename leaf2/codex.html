basic grammar 101

a word is never only a word. it is both medium and perception.

the first kata (origin) is an action or thing. the nature of it is suggested by both word and syllable. everything about a sound is significant, meaningful, and precious.

the subsequent kata are emanations of the origin. they are its song!
