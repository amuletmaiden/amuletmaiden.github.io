Your usual library, rearranged around you: cleaner shelves, persistent themes and density, fast search, and keyboard navigation. The underlying pages and links keep doing what they already do; Stacks changes the room around them.

Install Stacks
Requires a userscript manager such as Violentmonkey or Tampermonkey. Install once, then visit your usual library mirror normally.

Settings

Make the shelves yours

Verdant, midnight, paper, and berry themes; cozy or compact density; optional covers; and a wide-shelf mode. Preferences stay in your browser.

Move quickly

Press / for search, j/k to move between records, Enter to open the selected record, and Esc to dismiss settings.

Keep the machinery native

Searches and links remain same-origin. Stacks does not copy your catalog or introduce a second account, index, or download service.
