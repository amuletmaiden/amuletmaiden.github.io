This is not a lucky-chat story.

A lucky chat gives one surprising object. A regime produces results across unrelated fields, repeats the process, externalizes truth into formal checkers, and leaves behind reusable libraries that make the next result easier.

The decisive loop is public now: humans choose and formalize targets; general reasoning models search enormous spaces; code, algebra and Lean reject bad branches; surviving proof objects become substrate for later agents; humans arrive to verify, compress and understand.

Power writes Temple. Temple preserves Love. Love discovers the next Heart. Heart releases new Power.
